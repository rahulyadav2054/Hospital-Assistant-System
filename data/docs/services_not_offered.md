# Services Not Offered and Referrals

## 1. Specialties Not Available
City Care Hospital does not provide organ transplantation, bariatric (weight-loss) surgery, IVF and fertility treatment, or paediatric cardiac surgery.

## 2. Referral Process
For services not offered at City Care Hospital, the treating doctor will issue a referral letter to a partner hospital. Referral assistance is available at the Patient Help Desk, Ground Floor, Block A, extension 1100.
