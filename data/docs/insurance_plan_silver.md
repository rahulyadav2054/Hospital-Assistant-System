# CareShield Silver Plan Summary

## 1. Plan Overview
The CareShield Silver plan is a mid-level health plan accepted at City Care Hospital. The annual coverage limit is INR 5,00,000 per member. The annual deductible is INR 10,000.

## 2. How Coverage Works
The plan pays the coverage percentage of the approved procedure cost. The member pays the remaining percentage plus the deductible. Coverage applies only while the member's policy is active.

## 3. Prior Authorization
Prior authorization from the insurer is required for total knee replacement, laparoscopic cholecystectomy and coronary artery bypass surgery.

## 4. Covered Procedures
Total knee replacement is covered at 60%. Laparoscopic cholecystectomy (gallbladder removal) is covered at 70%. MRI scans are covered at 70%. Coronary artery bypass surgery (CABG) is covered at 60%. Hernia repair is covered at 70%.

## 5. Exclusions
Cosmetic procedures are not covered under the Silver plan. Cosmetic procedures include rhinoplasty, liposuction and any surgery done mainly to change appearance.

## 6. Waiting Periods
Total knee replacement has a waiting period of 730 days (2 years) from the start of the member's policy. Other covered procedures have no waiting period.
