# Laboratory Reports Guide

## 1. Sample Collection
The Laboratory is on the Ground Floor, Block A, and is open from 07:00 to 19:00 every day. Fasting tests such as fasting blood glucose and lipid profile require 8 to 10 hours of fasting (water is allowed).

## 2. Report Turnaround Times
Routine blood tests such as CBC and blood glucose are reported within 6 hours. HbA1c is reported within 24 hours. Culture tests take 48 to 72 hours.

## 3. Accessing Reports
Reports are available on the patient portal and through this assistant once they are verified by a pathologist. Printed copies can be collected from the Laboratory reception.

## 4. Reference Ranges
Each result is shown with a reference range. A result marked high or low means it is outside the laboratory's reference range. Only your doctor can interpret what a result means for your health; please discuss your results with your doctor.
