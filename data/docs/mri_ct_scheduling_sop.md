# MRI and CT Scan Scheduling Procedure

## 1. Scope
This procedure applies to all outpatient MRI and CT scans performed by the Radiology Department, Block B, First Floor.

## 2. How to Schedule an MRI Scan
1. Obtain a written referral (imaging order) from a City Care Hospital doctor or an external registered doctor.
2. Call the Radiology booking desk at extension 3300, or visit the Radiology reception (Block B, First Floor) between 08:00 and 18:00, Monday to Saturday.
3. Share your patient ID, the referral and your insurance details with the booking staff.
4. If your insurance plan requires prior authorization for MRI, the booking desk will submit the request; approval usually takes 2 working days.
5. You will receive an appointment date and time by SMS, along with preparation instructions.
6. Arrive 30 minutes before your scan time to complete the safety screening form.

## 3. How to Schedule a CT Scan
1. Obtain a referral (imaging order) from your doctor.
2. Book at the Radiology reception or by calling extension 3300.
3. If the CT scan uses contrast dye, a serum creatinine blood test from the last 30 days is required before the scan.
4. Arrive 20 minutes before your scan time.

## 4. MRI Preparation
Remove all metal objects, including jewellery, hair clips and watches, before the scan. Tell the technician if you have a pacemaker, cochlear implant, metal implant or aneurysm clip. For abdominal MRI, do not eat or drink for 4 hours before the scan. An MRI scan usually takes 30 to 45 minutes.

## 5. Reports
MRI and CT reports are usually available within 24 hours on the patient portal. Urgent scans are reported within 2 hours.
