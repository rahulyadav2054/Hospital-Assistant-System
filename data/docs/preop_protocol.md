# Pre-operative Testing Protocol

## 1. Purpose
This protocol lists the tests a patient must complete before elective surgery at City Care Hospital. The surgical team uses it to confirm the patient is ready for anaesthesia.

## 2. Required Tests for Major Elective Surgery
### 2.1 Standard Pre-operative Panel
For major elective surgery under general anaesthesia (for example laparoscopic cholecystectomy, joint replacement or hernia repair), the standard pre-operative panel is: Complete Blood Count (CBC), Electrocardiogram (ECG) and Chest X-ray.

### 2.2 Additional Tests
Patients with diabetes also need an HbA1c test. Patients taking blood thinners also need a coagulation profile (PT/INR). These additional tests are ordered by the surgeon when applicable.

## 3. Required Tests for Minor Procedures
For minor procedures under local anaesthesia, only a Complete Blood Count (CBC) is required.

## 4. Validity of Test Results
Pre-operative test results are valid for 30 days before the date of surgery. Tests older than 30 days must be repeated.

## 5. Completing Pre-operative Tests
Blood tests are done at the Laboratory, Ground Floor, Block A, from 07:00 to 19:00 without an appointment. ECG and Chest X-ray are done at the Diagnostics wing, Block B, Ground Floor, from 08:00 to 17:00. Surgery cannot be scheduled until all required tests are completed and reviewed by the anaesthetist.
