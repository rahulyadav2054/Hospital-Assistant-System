# Department Directory

## Cardiology
Cardiology treats heart conditions such as chest pain, high blood pressure, heart attack and irregular heartbeat. Location: Block A, Third Floor. Phone: extension 3100. OPD hours: 09:00–17:00, Monday to Saturday.

## Orthopedics
Orthopedics treats bones, joints and muscles, including knee pain, joint replacement, fractures and back pain. Location: Block B, Second Floor. Phone: extension 3200. OPD hours: 09:00–16:00, Monday to Saturday.

## Radiology
Radiology performs X-ray, ultrasound, CT and MRI scans. Location: Block B, First Floor. Phone: extension 3300. Hours: 08:00–18:00, Monday to Saturday.

## Endocrinology
Endocrinology treats diabetes, thyroid disorders and hormone problems. Location: Block A, Second Floor. Phone: extension 3400. OPD hours: 10:00–16:00, Monday to Friday.

## General Surgery
General Surgery performs operations such as gallbladder removal, hernia repair and appendix removal. Location: Block C, First Floor. Phone: extension 3500. OPD hours: 09:00–15:00, Monday to Saturday.

## General Medicine
General Medicine treats fever, infections, and common adult illnesses, and handles routine follow-ups. Location: Block A, First Floor. Phone: extension 3600. OPD hours: 08:00–20:00, Monday to Saturday.

## Laboratory
The Laboratory performs blood and urine tests. Location: Block A, Ground Floor. Phone: extension 3700. Hours: 07:00–19:00 daily.

## Emergency Department
The Emergency Department is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Location: Block A, Ground Floor. Emergency number: 108 or hospital extension 911.

## Billing and Insurance Desk
The Billing and Insurance Desk handles bills, insurance pre-authorization and claims. Location: Block A, Ground Floor. Phone: extension 1200. Hours: 08:00–20:00 daily.
