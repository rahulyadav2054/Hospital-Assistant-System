# Patient Rights and Privacy Policy

## 1. Access to Your Records
Every patient has the right to view their own medical records, lab results and appointments. Patients can access these through the patient portal, this assistant, or the Medical Records Office.

## 2. Who Can See Your Records
Your records can be seen only by you and the hospital staff directly involved in your care. Records are not shared with family members, employers or other patients without your written consent.

## 3. Sharing Records with Family
To allow a family member to access your records, submit a signed consent form at the Medical Records Office, Block A, Second Floor.

## 4. Complaints
Privacy complaints can be raised with the Patient Relations Office at extension 1500 or by email to privacy@citycare.example.
