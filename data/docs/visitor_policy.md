# Visitor Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains when and how family members and friends may visit patients at City Care Hospital. It balances patient rest, infection control and family support.

## 2. General Wards
### 2.1 General Ward Visiting Hours
General ward visiting hours are 10:00–13:00 and 16:00–20:00 every day, including public holidays. A maximum of three visitors may be at the bedside at one time.

### 2.2 Overnight Attendants
One adult attendant (18 years or older) may stay overnight with a patient in a general ward. The attendant must collect an attendant pass from the ward nurse station before 21:00.

## 3. Intensive Care Unit (ICU)
### 3.1 ICU Visitor Rules
A maximum of two visitors are allowed at the ICU bedside at one time. Visitors must wash their hands with sanitizer before entering and after leaving. Visitors must wear the gown provided at the ICU entrance.

### 3.2 ICU Visiting Hours
ICU visiting hours are 11:00–12:00 and 17:00–18:00 daily. Visits outside these hours are only allowed with permission from the ICU charge nurse. The ICU charge nurse can be reached at extension 2210.

### 3.3 Children in the ICU
Children under 12 years of age are not permitted to visit the ICU. Exceptions may be approved by the ICU consultant on duty for end-of-life situations.

## 4. Infection Control
Visitors with fever, cough, cold, vomiting or diarrhoea must not visit any patient. Flowers and outside food are not allowed in the ICU, the oncology ward or the neonatal unit.

## 5. Maternity and Neonatal Unit
The partner of a patient in the maternity ward may visit at any time. Other visitors may visit the maternity ward from 16:00–19:00. Only parents may enter the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU).
