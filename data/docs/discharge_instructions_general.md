# General Discharge Instructions

## 1. Before You Leave the Hospital
1. Your doctor will sign the discharge order during the morning ward round, usually by 11:00.
2. Collect your discharge summary from the ward nurse station. It lists your diagnosis, procedures done and medicines.
3. Collect discharge medicines from the In-patient Pharmacy, Ground Floor, Block A.
4. Complete billing clearance at the Billing Desk, Ground Floor, Block A. Insurance patients must wait for the insurer's final approval, which usually takes 2 to 4 hours.
5. Book your follow-up appointment at the department reception or through the patient portal.
6. Return your attendant pass to the ward nurse station.

## 2. Warning Signs After Discharge
Return to the Emergency Department or call 108 if you have fever above 101°F (38.3°C), increasing pain, bleeding or discharge from a wound, difficulty breathing, chest pain or fainting.

## 3. Discharge Time
The target discharge time is 14:00. Patients who stay after 14:00 for personal reasons may be charged for an extra half day.

## 4. Medical Records
A copy of your full medical records can be requested at the Medical Records Office, Block A, Second Floor. Copies are provided within 3 working days.
