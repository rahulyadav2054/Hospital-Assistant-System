# CareShield Basic Plan Summary

## 1. Plan Overview
The CareShield Basic plan is an entry-level health plan accepted at City Care Hospital. The annual coverage limit is INR 2,00,000 per member. The annual deductible is INR 15,000.

## 2. How Coverage Works
The plan pays the coverage percentage of the approved procedure cost. The member pays the remaining percentage plus the deductible. Coverage applies only while the member's policy is active.

## 3. Prior Authorization
Prior authorization from the insurer is required for laparoscopic cholecystectomy and MRI scans.

## 4. Covered Procedures
Laparoscopic cholecystectomy (gallbladder removal) is covered at 50%. MRI scans are covered at 50%. Hernia repair is covered at 50%. Total knee replacement and other joint replacement surgery are not covered under the Basic plan. Coronary artery bypass surgery (CABG) is not covered under the Basic plan.

## 5. Exclusions
Cosmetic procedures are not covered under the Basic plan. Cosmetic procedures include rhinoplasty, liposuction and any surgery done mainly to change appearance.

## 6. Waiting Periods
The Basic plan has no waiting period for the covered procedures listed in section 4.
