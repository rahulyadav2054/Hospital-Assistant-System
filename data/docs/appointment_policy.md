# Appointment Policy

## 1. Booking Appointments
Outpatient appointments can be booked through the patient portal, this assistant, or the central appointment desk at extension 1000 between 08:00 and 20:00, Monday to Saturday.

## 2. Rescheduling and Cancellation
Appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled up to 24 hours before the appointment time. Changes within 24 hours of the appointment must be made by calling the department directly. An appointment can be rescheduled to another available slot with the same doctor.

## 3. Late Arrival
Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may be asked to take the next available slot on the same day.

## 4. No-show Policy
Patients who miss three appointments in six months without notice will need to book through the central appointment desk only.

## 5. What to Bring
Bring your patient ID card, insurance card, previous reports and a list of current medicines to every appointment.
