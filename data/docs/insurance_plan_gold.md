# CareShield Gold Plan Summary

## 1. Plan Overview
The CareShield Gold plan is a comprehensive health plan accepted at City Care Hospital. The annual coverage limit is INR 10,00,000 per member. The annual deductible is INR 5,000.

## 2. How Coverage Works
The plan pays the coverage percentage of the approved procedure cost. The member pays the remaining percentage plus the deductible. Coverage applies only while the member's policy is active.

## 3. Prior Authorization
Prior authorization from the insurer is required for total knee replacement and coronary artery bypass surgery. The hospital's Billing and Insurance Desk submits prior authorization requests.

## 4. Covered Procedures
Total knee replacement is covered at 80%. Laparoscopic cholecystectomy (gallbladder removal) is covered at 90%. MRI scans are covered at 90%. Coronary artery bypass surgery (CABG) is covered at 80%. Hernia repair is covered at 90%.

## 5. Exclusions
Cosmetic procedures are not covered under the Gold plan. Cosmetic procedures include rhinoplasty, liposuction and any surgery done mainly to change appearance.

## 6. Waiting Periods
The Gold plan has no waiting period for the covered procedures listed in section 4.
